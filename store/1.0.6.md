## What's New in This Version

**Strong's, rebuilt word by word**

Every word of the KJV is now linked to the Hebrew or Greek word it translates,
from the King James Version's own texts: the Hebrew Masoretic text and the
Textus Receptus. When the KJV splits one Hebrew word across a verse ("And God
saw"), its pieces are linked as one. The small words the Hebrew writes on its
words ("and", "in", "his") go with the word they belong to. The words the
translators supplied stay in italics, and every other word is underlined:
solid where it has a Strong's number, dotted where the Hebrew word has none.
The KJV usage counts are the KJV's own.

**Interlinear**

See any verse in its Hebrew or Greek, word by word: each word with its
transliteration and full parsing ("Verb · Qal · Perfect · 3rd masculine
singular"). Hebrew reads from the right. Tap a word to see its definition
under it. Open it from any Strong's entry, or select a verse and tap Parse.
Small Hebrew words with no Strong's number of their own, such as lo ("unto
him"), have an entry too.

**Search the dictionary and Strong's**

Search now has tabs for verses, Webster's 1828 dictionary and Strong's.
Results come as you type, and your recent searches are kept. Words that
Webster's 1828 doesn't have come from Wiktionary.

**More commentaries built in**

The Scofield Reference Notes, Spurgeon's Treasury of David, F. B. Meyer's
Through the Bible Day by Day and Clarence Larkin's Dispensational Truth now
come with the app, alongside the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge.

**Verse Feed**

A new place in Study Tools to swipe through well-known verses, one at a time.
Double-tap a verse to add it to Memory Verses.

**Fixes and improvements**

• Choose the version when adding a memory verse
• A number named in a Strong's definition opens in a window of its own
• Eight Strong's entries corrected, and a clear message when an entry doesn't
  exist
• Content no longer sits a status bar too high on some screens
• "Copied" and "Bookmarked" show on a backing you can see, and slide away
  with the bar
• Updated to the latest app framework

## Before submitting

- **Push to GitHub first, and let Pages finish deploying.** The push publishes
  the website with the same changes, including the bundled data (the
  Treasury of Scripture Knowledge download its licence asks for stays at
  brockigordon1611.github.io/Scriptorium/bundled/tske.json).
- **The app must stay free**, and the TSK copyright notice must travel with it
  (About & Legal and the commentary's info page). Unchanged.
- **Credits:** STEPBible's Hebrew and Greek data (CC BY 4.0) and Wiktionary
  (CC BY-SA 4.0) are credited in About & Legal; the four new commentaries are
  public domain and listed there.
- **Privacy:** nothing new is collected or synced. One thing to consider: a
  dictionary search for a word Webster's 1828 doesn't have sends that word
  (nothing about the person) to Wiktionary. The policy's "we do not share your
  data" still holds, but a sentence saying so would be more open. Optional.
- **Screenshots:** the Interlinear, the Read page with every word underlined
  and its Strong's entry, Search's Dictionary and Strong's tabs, and Verse
  Feed would all make good new shots. The Strong's panel has changed enough
  that an old shot of it misrepresents the app.
- **Description:** worth a line each for the Interlinear, the rebuilt Strong's
  and the new commentaries, if the description lists features.
- Version and build are set in Xcode (1.0.6, build 7).
