# App Store Connect — Scriptorium 1.0.5 (build 6)

Previous release: 1.0.4 (build 5). 39 commits since.

---

## What's New in This Version

**Commentaries, with the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge**

A new Commentaries section in Study Tools, with the Treasury of Scripture
Knowledge built in: cross-references for every verse of the Bible. It opens on
the chapter you are reading, follows your Scripture Size, and goes full screen
as you scroll. Every reference can be tapped to preview the verse, then opened
in Read, and tapping a verse shows it in Read as well. Each chapter carries its
book's introduction. You can also import your own e-Sword commentaries.

**Memory Verses**

Add any passage and learn it by heart four ways: a flashcard that turns over,
first letters only, fill in the blanks over four rounds, or type it out and see
exactly which words you missed. Your verses and how often you have practiced
them sync to your account.

**Strong's, redesigned**

The Strong's panel has been laid out afresh: the word, its transliteration and
its definition in one card, then every way the KJV translates it, with the
verses a tap away. Words the translators supplied appear in italics, words left
untranslated are marked, and references are larger and easier to tap — each
opens the verse right where you are.

**Copy the way you like**

Settings → Copy Format sets how copied verses look: the reference above, below
or left off; verse numbers superscript, plain or hidden; a line per verse or one
paragraph; and the version name and quotation marks if you want them. A live
preview shows the result.

**Easier on the eyes**

Parallel, Bookmarks, the verse previews and the bookmark form share the new
Commentaries look, and their text now tracks Scripture Size, so moving between
Read and Study no longer means changing the text size. Recent Passages groups
your reading by day, with the time of each visit. Lists fade softly at their
edges, scrolling ends in a gentler bounce, and panels give more room to what is
in them.

**Fixes**

• Phrase search respects Partial Match, and whole-word search works with
  accented letters
• e-Sword files can be chosen on iPhone and iPad instead of appearing greyed out
• The Strong's panel covers the bottom bar rather than sitting behind it
• Compare's add and edit forms no longer tuck under the status bar
• A haze beneath the status bar on iOS 26 is gone
• Launch no longer waits on audio setup
• Bookmarks, highlights and recent passages load even when the rest of your
  data is slow to arrive
• Compatibility with the latest version of iOS

## Before submitting

- **Push to GitHub first, and let Pages finish deploying.** The Treasury of
  Scripture Knowledge is licensed on condition that the text is offered in an
  open format by download; that is `tske.json` at
  brockigordon1611.github.io/Scriptorium/bundled/tske.json, which is not live
  until this is pushed (it returns 404 today). The push also publishes the
  updated privacy policy. Check that URL loads before submitting.
- **The app must stay free**, and the TSK copyright notice must travel with it
  (it is in About & Legal and on the commentary's info page). Flag it if pricing
  or in-app purchase ever comes up.
- **Privacy:** memory verses are new data synced to the user's account, the same
  kind as bookmarks and highlights (App Privacy → User Content → Other User
  Content, linked to the user, App Functionality). If that category is already
  declared, nothing changes. The policy now lists memory verses and recent
  passages, with a new effective date.
- **Screenshots:** Commentaries and Memory Verses are both new and would make
  good shots; the Strong's panel has changed enough that an old shot of it
  misrepresents the app.
- **Description:** worth a line each for Commentaries (with the TSK) and Memory
  Verses if the description lists features.
- Version and build are set in Xcode (1.0.5, build 6).
