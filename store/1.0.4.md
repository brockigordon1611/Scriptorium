# App Store Connect — Scriptorium 1.0.4 (build 5)

Previous release: 1.0.3 (build 4). 91 commits since.

---

## What's New in This Version

**Search, rebuilt**

Results now appear as you type, with a running count of the verses and
occurrences that matched. Search takes the full screen so more of the text is in
view, and the bar above the results holds everything you need: a wheel to jump
to any book in the results, scope filters for the whole Bible or just the Old or
New Testament, and modes for all words, an exact phrase, or any word. Case
Sensitive and Partial Match are there too, with a one-tap reset.

**A new Scripture Atlas**

All fourteen maps have been replaced with seventeen new plates, scanned from
period originals at four times the resolution. They run in the order the story
does — from the descendants of Noah and the land promised to Abraham, through
the division among the twelve tribes, to the journeys of Christ and the
apostles. Zoom now goes twice as deep, on the Larkin charts as well, and place
names stay legible all the way in.

**Text size, everywhere**

Two separate controls under Reading Appearance. Scripture Size now reaches 60
for the verse text. Menus & Buttons scales the rest of the app — navigation,
labels, buttons and panels — from 85% to 160%, so you can pair large scripture
with a compact interface, or the reverse. A Reset Appearance Settings button
puts it all back.

**Fixes**

• Highlighted search results no longer show stray markup around the match
• Zoomed maps and charts stay sharp instead of softening
• The opening animation runs once, smoothly, instead of restarting partway
• The Strong's panel close button stays in reach without scrolling back up
• Reading plan links land on the first verse of the passage, even when you
  are already in that chapter, and no longer open the verse menu
• A verse you landed on by link takes one tap to act on, not two
• Audio playback speed carries across chapters
• You stay signed in through longer sessions
• Compatibility with the latest version of iOS
