Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Scriptorium is a personal Bible reading and study app. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and send authentication emails (account confirmation, password reset).

We do not collect your name, phone number, payment information, location, or any other personal data.

How Your Information Is Used

Your email address is used solely to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Send account confirmation and password reset emails
  • Sync your reading data (bookmarks, notes, uploaded Bible versions) across your devices

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, or marketing purposes. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Data Storage

Account and sync data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted database platform. Data is stored in the United States. Supabase's privacy practices are described at supabase.com/privacy.

User-Uploaded Content

Scriptorium allows you to upload personal Bible files (such as e-Sword modules) that you have legally obtained. This content is stored in your private account and is not accessible to other users or shared with anyone.

Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app's Settings. Upon deletion, your email address and all synced data are permanently removed from our servers.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to brockigordon@icloud.com.
