Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Tap Sign In on the home screen, then choose Create Account. Enter your email address and a password. You'll receive a confirmation email — tap the link inside to activate your account.

How do I reset my password?

On the Sign In screen, tap Forgot Password and enter your email address. A reset link will be sent to your inbox.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data from our servers.

How do I upload a Bible file?

Scriptorium supports e-Sword Bible modules (.bblx) that you have legally obtained. In the app, go to Library → Add Bible and select your file. Uploaded files are stored privately in your account and synced across your devices.

My data isn't syncing — what should I do?

  • Make sure you are signed in to your account.
  • Check that your device has an active internet connection.
  • Try closing and reopening the app.
  • If the problem persists, contact us at brockigordon@icloud.com.

For anything not covered above, email us at brockigordon@icloud.com.
